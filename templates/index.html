SAIL Fleet Advisory
Core Mission: Spot to Multi-Voyage Transition

Historically, SAIL relies heavily on daily reactive spot exploration. Our optimization models prove that securing 3–6 voyage Consecutive Voyage Contracts (CVC) or Medium-Term COA yields 8.5% to 12.8% net freight savings and mitigates seasonal monsoon squeeze.

Vessel Upsizing & Draft Maximization

Discharging Capesize vessels at deepwater ports (Gangavaram & Dhamra: 18m–20m draft) slashes landed freight to $14–$18/MT compared to Supramax ($24–$28/MT). For Haldia, automated Sandheads lighterage yields superior unit economics over direct Handysize shipping.

Demurrage Risk Mitigation

Paradip port pre-berthing wait times peak at 3.5 days during post-monsoon restocking. Algorithmic chartering recommends specifying reversible laytime and 25,000+ TPD guaranteed discharge clauses in mid-term tenders.

This dossier presents the algorithmic determination for raw material vessel chartering and bulk freight hedging. In accordance with the mandate to move from multiple single spot market fixtures to short-term and medium-term multiple voyage contracts (COA), the quantitative model recommends executing a Medium-Term Multiple Voyage Contract (COA) for the upcoming bi-annual lifting schedule.

2. VESSEL SELECTION & PORT RESTRICTION COMPLIANCE

Physical compliance evaluation confirms that Dhamra Port's 18.0m permissible draft accommodates fully laden Capesize vessels without requiring offshore lighterage. High mechanized discharge capacity (45,000 TPD) restricts port stay to 3.3 days, limiting demurrage exposure.

4. RISK MITIGATION, WEATHER & IDLE TIME CONTROLS

  • Bay of Bengal Seasonal Controls: Pre-scheduled laycan windows to avoid peak cyclone depression clusters in October–November.
  • Reversible Laytime Clause: Charter party terms must enforce reversible laytime between loading terminal (e.g. DBCT) and discharge berth to absorb queueing shocks.
  • Triangulated Ballasting: Recommends backhaul fixtures with iron ore/fines to East Asia to minimize shipowner deadhead ballast cost, securing competitive freight bids for SAIL.
